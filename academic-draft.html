About Me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Saskatchewan. I received my PhD in Mathematics from the University of Alberta in 2019 under the supervision of Manish M. Patnaik.

Previously, I was a Hill Assistant Professor at Rutgers University (2020–2023). My academic work includes mathematical research, university teaching, mentoring, and outreach.

Research Interests

My research focuses on infinite-dimensional Lie algebras and groups, combinatorial and geometric group theory, and their connections to number theory and representation theory.

Kac–Moody Groups Lie Theory Representation Theory Geometric Group Theory Number Theory
